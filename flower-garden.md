# Flower Garden

Synthetic field, entered through `[Gen 9] Flower Garden` or `[Gen 9] Doubles Flower Garden` (also available in the generated FFA and Multi field lists).

Stages: `FLOWERGARDEN1` through `FLOWERGARDEN5`. Stage 1 announces: `Seeds line the field.`

When a Flower Garden stage is the base field, ordinary field generation, replacement, and clearing are blocked. Each transition replaces its effects and supplied background: no inheritance or stacking of previous fields.

## Stage 1

- No Grass damage multiplier.
- Flower Gift activates without sun, including Cherrim's Sunshine form and its usual Attack/Sp. Def modifiers.
- Swarm boosts Bug moves at any HP.
- Growth raises the user's Attack and Sp. Atk by two stages.
- Rototiller raises grounded Grass Pokemon's Attack and Sp. Atk by two stages, and includes its grounded user regardless of type.
- Nature Power calls Growth on its user.
- Secret Power becomes Sweet Scent entirely: no damage, lowers opposing Pokemon's evasion by two stages, and spends Secret Power PP.
- Mimicry changes the holder to Grass.
- Synthetic Seed is consumed, raises Sp. Def by one stage, applies Ingrain, and advances to Stage 2.

## Later Stages

| Effect | Stage 2 | Stage 3 | Stage 4 | Stage 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grass damage | 1.1x | 1.3x | 1.5x | 2x |
| Bug damage | normal | 1.5x | 2x | 2x |
| Fire damage | normal | 1.5x | 1.5x | 1.5x |
| Flower moves | normal | 1.2x | 1.5x | 1.5x |
| Overgrow | threshold 2/3 HP | normal | always, 1.8x | always, 2x |
| Ingrain | 1/8 HP | normal | 1/4 HP | normal |
| Infestation | normal | 1/6 HP | 1/4 HP | 1/3 HP |

Flower moves: Fleur Cannon, Flower Trick, Petal Blizzard, Petal Dance, Springtide Storm. Flower/type boosts stack. Cut gains 1.5x damage at Stages 2-5, replaced by 2x against Grass.

- Stage 2: Harvest always activates; Leaf Guard activates; Flower Shield grants all allies +1 Defense/Sp. Def, regardless of type.
- Stage 3: Flower Veil protects holder/allies with 25% damage reduction; Sweet Scent lowers Defense/Sp. Def/Evasion by one; Flower Shield grants all allies +2 Defense/Sp. Def; Floral Healing restores full HP.
- Stages 3-5: Lingering Aroma lowers one random Defense/Sp. Def/Speed stat per opponent by one each turn.
- Syrup keeps its evasion drop. Stage 3 adds Defense/Sp. Def; Stage 4 also adds Attack/Sp. Atk; Stage 5 also adds Speed/Accuracy. All drops are one stage.
- Stage 4: Chlorophyll activates.
- Stages 4-5: Grass's contribution to weaknesses becomes neutral, preserving the other type's matchups. Grassy Glide gets +1 priority.
- Stage 5: Petal Dance, Petal Blizzard and opponent-targeting powders hit both foes, not allies. Rage Powder stays normal. Nature Power calls Petal Blizzard; Secret Power becomes Petal Dance, including locking/confusion.

## Transitions

Successful Growth, Flower Shield, Rain Dance, Sunny Day, Rototiller, Ingrain, Grassy Terrain, Water Sport and Bloom Doom grow one stage, capped at 5. Message: `The garden grew a little!`

Switch-in growth: Drizzle, Drought, Flower Gift, Flower Veil, Orichalcum Pulse, Mega Sol/Searing Petals, Seed Sower, Grassy Surge, Forest Surge, Pollen Bloom, Toxic Bloom and Ancient Bloom. Seed Sower's damage-triggered terrain effect grows the field too. Grassy/Forest generation by these triggers never replaces it.

Ripen on the Pokemon causing growth makes it +2 at Stages 1-3. Cut/X-Scissor reduce one stage, stopping at 1. Breaking Swipe does not cut it. Acid Downpour resets it to Stage 1.

## Burning

Stages 3-5 ignite from Heat Wave, Eruption, Searing Shot, Flame Burst, Lava Plume, Fire Pledge, Mind Blown, Incinerate, Burning Jealousy or Inferno Overdrive. Rain and Water Sport block ignition. Message: `The garden caught fire!`

Garden-origin Burning Field retains the field-generation lock. Each completed turn reduces its recoverable stage by one. Same-turn dousing restores the ignition stage. When nothing remains, it automatically returns to Stage 1 with `The garden was burned to ash` and can grow again.

Rain extinguishes it immediately. Rain Dance, Water Spout, Water Sport and Drizzle also grow the restored garden. Other existing Burning Field extinguishing moves restore it without growth. Sandstorm does not clear garden-origin fire.
